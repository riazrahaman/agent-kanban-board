A normal board assumes a human is watching

Agents stall

A process dies holding a card. On a human board it sits there until somebody notices — hours later.

Agents collide

Two workers take the same task. Both do the work. One silently overwrites the other.

Agents skip

A builder marks its own work done. Nothing reviewed it, nothing tested it, and the board says DONE.

Every one of these is a convention on a human board. A convention is something an agent will break at 3am, confidently, in parallel, forty times.

Frame the problem before the solution. On a human Kanban board the rules are social: you don't take someone's card, you don't skip review, you tell people when you're blocked. Agents do not honour conventions. They honour what the API refuses to do. That is the whole design thesis.
