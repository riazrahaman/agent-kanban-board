reclaims on the live board — stalls that cleared themselves instead of waiting for me to notice

A background reaper returns a card to READY for three distinct reasons:

The lease expired

The holder stopped renewing. Usually a dead process.

No progress past the stall window

Still holding the lease, but nothing logged for 30 minutes. A live agent stuck in a loop.

Active with no owner at all

Someone PATCHed a status without claiming. Normalized after a grace window.

reapExpiredClaims · KANBAN_PROGRESS_STALL_MS 1800000 · KANBAN_ORPHAN_GRACE_MS 300000

Resist the shorthand "every reclaim is a dead agent" — it is wrong and an engineer will catch it. The second cause is the interesting one: an agent that is alive, holding its lease, and achieving nothing. The board treats silence as failure. Optional line: nobody noticed that agent died; the board did.
