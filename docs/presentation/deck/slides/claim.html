Claim first. Then hold a lease.

Only a READY card can be claimed

The claim sets an owner and starts a lease. Ten minutes by default.

Work renews the lease

Every log line and heartbeat re-arms it. An agent that is working never loses its card.

A status-only PATCH is not a claim

It leaves the card active with no owner — and the reaper resets it to READY.

The claim

POST /api/tasks/demo-01/claim
  ?project=demo

X-API-Token: tok-build
X-Agent-Role: builder

{ "agent_id": "builder-1",
  "lease_ms": 60000 }

READY → IN_PROGRESS, owner set, clock started.

KANBAN_CLAIM_TTL_MS 600000 · clamped between KANBAN_MIN_LEASE_MS and KANBAN_MAX_LEASE_MS

The lease is the heart of the design. An agent does not promise to finish; it holds a time-boxed right to the card and keeps renewing it by doing visible work. Note the third card specially: a status-only PATCH into an active stage is the most common integration mistake, and the board corrects it rather than trusting it.
